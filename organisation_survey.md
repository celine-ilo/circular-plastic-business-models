# Electronic survey to organisations incorporating digital innovation

Administered via Typeform to start-ups managing plastic waste. Sent to 39
organisations identified through literature review and databases; 17
completed it.

## A. Background

1. What is the name of your company or innovation?
2. What year was your organisation founded?
3. Location (city, country)
4. Total quantity of plastic waste processed to date (kg)
5. Quantity of plastic waste processed in the last 12 months (kg)
6. Which of these areas does your work focus on? *Multiple answer:* reuse,
   reduce, recover or collection, upcycle, recycle

## B. Business

7. How has your business been funded? *Multiple answer:* bootstrapping
   (savings, friends or family), grants, loans (bank, microfinance,
   convertible and similar), crowdfunding, venture capital, business
   incubator or accelerator, other
8. In your opinion, after how many years can a start-up in this sector be
   deemed ready for scale-up investment, meaning sustainable profits and not
   dependent on grants? 0-1 year, 1-2 years, 3-5 years, 5-7 years, other
9. What unique opportunities in terms of circular plastic management exist in
   your sector? Unsaturated market, government incentives, market demand,
   inadequate infrastructure
10. How are digital innovations used in your business?
11. What are the opportunities for scaling up?
12. What are the barriers to scaling up?
13. Rank these barriers to scaling, 1 being most significant: funding,
    regulation, local capacity, saturated market, other
14. Rank these barriers to mass adoption of digital tools in plastic waste
    management, 1 being most significant
