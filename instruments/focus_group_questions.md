# Focus group discussion guide

Used across 16 focus group sessions with stakeholders in Namibia, Zambia,
Nigeria, Kenya, Uganda and Rwanda, June to October 2020. Participants were
drawn from five categories: innovators and start-ups, academic researchers,
government representatives, civil society, and investors.

1. Overall information about the organisation you represent (location, size,
   turnover, number of staff, plastics collected and processed, and so on)
2. What are the main challenges for transitioning to a circular economy?
3. What are the opportunities for transitioning to a circular economy?
4. How can digital innovations help solve the challenges?
5. What innovations for plastic waste management are you aware of?
6. How do you, as stakeholders, use digital technologies?
7. What are the opportunities and barriers to scaling up?
   Prompt for technical, social, political and economic factors.

Reproduced from Appendix 1 of the published paper.
