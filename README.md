# Digitally enabled business models for a circular plastic economy in Africa

Research instruments and study design from a qualitative study of circular
plastic business models across six African countries.

This repository holds the data collection instruments and a summary of the
method, so that the study can be understood, evaluated or replicated in
another setting. The underlying interview and focus group data are not held
here; the paper states that data are available on request from the authors.

## The study

Sixteen focus group sessions were conducted between June and October 2020
with stakeholders in Namibia, Zambia, Nigeria, Kenya, Uganda and Rwanda.
Participants came from five categories: innovators and start-ups, academic
researchers, government representatives, civil society, and investors.

Alongside these, a semi-structured electronic questionnaire went to 39
organisations identified through literature review and databases as active in
digital innovation for waste management. Seventeen completed it.

Transcripts were analysed in NVivo 12 using the six-step thematic analysis
procedure of Braun and Clarke (2006): initial reading and note-taking
captured as vignettes, mapping of vignettes to first-order themes, review of
first-order themes into a thematic map, refinement against aggregate
dimensions, and selection of extracts to explicate the resulting themes.

## Why these are worth having separately

A published appendix is hard to find, hard to quote from and impossible to
adapt. Research instruments are among the most reusable outputs of a
qualitative study, and a group running a comparable study in another region
would otherwise rebuild them from scratch.
